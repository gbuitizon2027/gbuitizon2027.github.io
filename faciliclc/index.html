FaciliCLC

To make reserving facilities a little less frustrating.

What is this?

Welcome to the initial prototype of FaciliCLC ! This website serves to display the reserved times of different facilities in CLC to help scholars pick the right times to reserve for their events and activites. What I've created right now is just the bare minimum (hihi sorry), but I would love for this website to handle multiple bookings during one timeslot (the table started to look ugly) and to be able to display more than just the current week, among other improvements. Thank you for taking the time to view my output!

How do I use it?

Ideally, only teachers, administrators, or trusted Sangguniang Mag-aaral Staff can add or edit bookings. However, for this version, I will let you try adding your own bookings! Simply click the blue button below to add a new booking, and input the necessary information.
